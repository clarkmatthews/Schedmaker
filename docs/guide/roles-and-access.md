# Roles and access

Roles decide what each person can see and change **in this company**. The same person can have a different role in a different company.

Open **Company settings → Roles** to view or edit them. Assign a role to a person on the Employees page.

## None, View, and Edit

Every area of the app has one of three levels:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **None** | Hidden. The menu item does not appear and the page redirects away. |
| **View** | Can open the page and read it. Cannot save changes. |
| **Edit** | Can change that area. |

Areas include Employees, Scheduling, Create companies, and each settings section (Company, Hours, Scheduling rules, MMS, Teams, Responsibilities, Roles).

**Create companies** Edit lets someone start another workplace and shows **Switch Company** at the bottom of the hamburger so they can return to Your companies. A person with no companies yet can still create their first one.

Scheduling **View** is special: that person only sees **their own published shifts**. Scheduling **Edit** sees the full calendar, drafts, unassigned shifts, and labor totals.

## Default roles

Every new company starts with three roles:

| Role | Typical access |
|---|---|
| **Employee** | Scheduling View only — own published shifts and My account |
| **Restaurant manager** | Employees Edit and Scheduling Edit |
| **Administrator** | Edit on every area |

You can create more roles (for example a payroll viewer who can see Employees but not change the schedule). Custom roles use the same None / View / Edit grid.

## Administrator is protected

The Administrator role always has full Edit access. You cannot weaken it, and you cannot delete it.

Other protections:

- The company must keep at least one person in the Administrator role.
- You cannot change your own role.
- Assigning Administrator to someone requires permission to **edit Roles**, not only Employees.
- You cannot delete a role that still has people in it. Move them first.

## Support staff

Internal Schedmaker support accounts can open every company with full access. They are not a role you assign on the Employees page.

## What people land on

When someone opens a company, Schedmaker sends them to the first area they can use: Employees if they can see it, otherwise the first team’s schedule, otherwise Settings, otherwise My account.
