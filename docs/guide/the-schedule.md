# The schedule

Open a team from the hamburger menu, then choose **Scheduling**. This is the calendar for that team.

![Week calendar](../screenshots/week-view.png)

## Week and day

**Week** shows seven days across. **Day** shows one date as a timeline, with 15-minute rows and a per-hour footer so you can see who is on the floor at a given time.

- Previous / Next move by a week in week view, or by a day in day view.
- Click a day header in week view to jump to that day.
- The clock next to the date uses the company time zone.

![Day calendar](../screenshots/day-view.png)

## View by employee or job

**View by employee** is a row per person. Managers also see an **Unassigned** row for open shifts.

**View by job** is a row per job, plus **No job** for shifts that do not have a position yet.

The same shifts appear in both views — only the grouping changes.

## Creating and editing a shift

If your role can edit the schedule, click an empty cell to create a shift, or click a shift bar to edit it.

In the shift window you can set:

- The person and the job
- One or more days (useful for repeating the same times)
- Start and stop on the 15-minute grid
- Breaks
- Optional responsibilities (if the company turned that feature on)
- Whether this shift is **published**

A shift cannot be longer than 23 hours. Times have to fall inside the company’s hours template for that weekday, and you cannot put a shift on a closed day.

Click **Save** to write the change. Existing shifts can also be copied to other days, moved by dragging, or deleted.

## Breaks

Breaks are unpaid time inside the shift. They show as a hatch on the bar. On-clock hours (and labor cost) subtract break time.

Each break is at least 15 minutes, lines up to the 15-minute grid, stays inside the shift, and cannot overlap another break. You can place two breaks back-to-back.

## Draft vs published

New shifts start as **drafts**. Employees do not see drafts. Managers see every shift.

- **Publish day** or **Publish week** makes every shift in the visible range visible to the assigned people.
- **Unpublish** hides them again.
- You can also publish a single shift in the shift window.

Publishing a future assigned shift can send the person an email. Publishing a week can also send a picture of the schedule by text if MMS is turned on.

## Copy last week

The copy icon on the calendar copies last week (or the same weekday last week, in day view) onto the dates you are looking at.

This **replaces** what is already there. It does not merge. You will get a “clear and replace” confirmation first. Shifts for deactivated people are skipped.

## Print week

**Print week** opens a printer-friendly page in a new tab. Employees who open it only see their own published shifts.

## What employees see

Someone with schedule **View** (the default Employee role) sees:

- Only their own **published** shifts
- Only their own row
- No labor totals, no hourly rates, and no edit buttons

They can still use week/day, print, and the **?** help on the controls they have.

## Unassigned shifts

Open shifts live on the **Unassigned** row (when viewing by employee). They are visible to managers only. Assign a person in the shift window when you know who will work it.
