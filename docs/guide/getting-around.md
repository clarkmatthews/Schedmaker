# Getting around

## Signing in

The home page is the sign-in screen. Use the email and password for your account. After you sign in, Schedmaker takes you to **Your companies**.

If you do not have an account yet, use **Sign up**. You will get an activation email. Open the link, set your name, phone, and password, and the account becomes active.

If you forgot your password, use **Reset**. Schedmaker emails a link that expires after a couple of hours. After you set a new password, other devices that were signed in will need to sign in again.

If a manager added you as an employee, you may get an email (and a text if they entered your phone number). Use the activation link in that message the first time you sign in.

## Your companies

This is the first screen after login. Each card is a company you belong to. The number under the name is how many teams that company has.

- Click a company to go to the first page your role allows — usually Employees, then Scheduling, then Settings.
- **New company** creates a separate workplace. You become its Administrator, and it starts with one team.

A company is the whole workplace. A team is a group you schedule inside that workplace. Most people work in one company; some managers work in more than one.

## The menu

The hamburger icon (three lines) in the top-left opens the menu.

When you are inside a company, the menu can show:

- **Employees** — the people directory (if your role can see it)
- Each **team**, with **Scheduling** underneath
- **Company settings** — hours, roles, teams, and other company setup
- **My account** — your name, password, and calendar feed
- **Log out**
- **Switch Company** — back to Your companies (if your role can create companies)

When you are not inside a company (on Your companies or My account), the menu lists the companies you can open.

Items you do not have permission to see are hidden. That is expected — an Employee will not see Employees or Company settings.

The Schedmaker logo always takes you back to Your companies.

## My account

**My account** is about you, not about the company.

- Update your name, email, or phone.
- Change your password. This signs other devices out.
- Copy your **iCal feed** URL if you want published shifts in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook. Anyone who has that link can see your published shifts, so treat it like a password.

You cannot change your own role from this page. A manager with the right permission does that on the Employees page.
