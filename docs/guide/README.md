# Schedmaker guide

Schedmaker is the place you build and share employee schedules. A manager can lay out a week or a single day, assign people and jobs, publish the plan, and see overtime and meal-break warnings as they work. An employee can sign in and see their own published shifts.

This guide is written for people who use the app, not for developers. It explains what each page does and why the app behaves the way it does.

## How the app is organized

A **company** is the workplace you schedule for — one restaurant, shop, or location. Inside a company you have **teams** (the groups you actually schedule, such as Front of House). **People** belong to the company, and they can be assigned to one or more teams.

What you see after you sign in depends on your **role**. An Administrator can reach everything. A Restaurant manager can usually manage people and the schedule. An Employee can see their published shifts and their own account.
