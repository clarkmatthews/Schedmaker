# People

The **Employees** page is the company directory. Open it from the hamburger menu. Click a name to open that person’s details on the right.

If you do not see Employees in the menu, your role does not include that area.

## What you can set for a person

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| Name, email, phone | How they sign in and how Schedmaker contacts them. After they activate their account, they manage name, email, and phone themselves on My account. |
| Role | What they can see and change in this company. See [Roles and access](roles-and-access.md). |
| Hourly rate | Used to estimate labor cost on the schedule. People without a rate still appear on the calendar; they just do not add to estimated dollars. |
| Meal-break waiver | When meal rules are on, this can suppress the first-meal warning on shorter shifts. It does not remove the break from the shift. |
| Teams | Which team calendars they appear on. A person can be on more than one team. |
| Internal ID | An optional code you use (employee number, POS ID, and so on). |

New people start with the **Employee** role unless you pick a different one.

## Adding someone

Use the create form on the Employees page. If that email or phone already belongs to a Schedmaker account, they are added to this company instead of creating a second login.

When you add someone, they can get an email invite and, if you entered a phone number, a text. They use the link to activate and set a password.

## Roles on a person

Changing someone’s role changes what the menu shows them and what they can edit.

A few limits:

- You cannot change **your own** role.
- The company must keep at least one **Administrator**.
- Giving someone the Administrator role requires permission to edit Roles, not only Employees.

## Deactivated people

Deactivating hides someone from new scheduling and from the default employee list. It does not delete their account or their past shifts.

- Use the deactivated filter on the Employees page to find them again.
- You can reactivate them later.
- You cannot deactivate yourself.
- Their existing shifts stay on the calendar, but they show as unassigned so you can give the work to someone else.

Deactivate is the way to take someone off the roster. There is no “delete employee” that erases history.

## Teams and jobs

Teams and jobs are set up under **Company settings**, not on the employee row.

- A **team** is a schedule (Front of House, Kitchen, and so on).
- A **job** is a position on that team (Server, Cook). Jobs have a color that shows up on shift bars.

Adding someone to a team puts them on that team’s calendar rows. Assigning a job to a shift is separate — you pick the job when you create or edit the shift.
